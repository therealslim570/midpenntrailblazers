Smith’s Knob Scramble

A rugged out-and-back over Smith’s Knob on the Loyalsock Trail, with an 8-mile challenge and a shorter Half Scramble.

8 MILES + HALF SCRAMBLESaturday, June 13, 20269:00 am

About the race

Earn the view. Embrace the scramble.

The Smith’s Knob Scramble is organized by a group that is a friend of Mid Penn Trailblazers; it is not hosted by our club. The event supports the Hughesville Cross Country team. The race follows the Loyalsock Trail over steep Smith’s Knob: the main course is 8 miles, with a shorter Half Scramble of roughly 4.5 miles for runners and hikers who prefer to skip the knob. The shorter course still includes climbing and technical trail.

The race has limited parking at Camp Conley, and the organizers encourage carpooling. Past race proceeds have also supported the Think Big pediatric cancer fund.

Course and planning notes

  • Camp Conley is about 15 miles north of Montoursville.
  • Parking is limited; carpooling is encouraged.
  • Expect steep climbs and technical trail. Check the current race page for directions and course updates.
