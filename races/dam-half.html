Dam Half

A challenging forest trail half marathon, with rocky climbs, stream crossings, and scenic views—and a shorter trail option.

HALF MARATHON + SHORT COURSESunday, September 27, 20268:00 am

About the race

A proper Pennsylvania trail day.

The Dam Half is a challenging trail half marathon in the forests of Central Pennsylvania. Its roughly 13-mile route combines single- and double-track trail with sections of forest road, technical rocky ground, roots, mud, downed trees, and small stream crossings. The organizer describes roughly 2,300 feet of elevation gain.

There are demanding climbs and descents, with scenic vistas before the final downhill to the finish. The club’s published 2026 schedule also lists a roughly four-mile trail option. Review the official event information for current course details, aid station cutoffs, and participant guidance before signing up.

Know before you go

  • This is a technical trail course, not a rail-trail event.
  • Follow the event’s trail markings and attend the pre-race briefing.
  • Check the official race information for current cutoff and safety details.
