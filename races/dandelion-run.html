About the race

5K or 10K at Camp Mount Luther.

The Dandelion Run is an entry-level 5K and 10K trail race at Camp Mount Luther in Mifflinburg. Participants decide on race day whether to run the 5K or do a second loop for the 10K. The race benefits Camp Mount Luther and was named in memory of Rev. Glenn Heasley, whose favorite “flower” was the dandelion.

The course is marked, includes one water stop, and is electronically timed. A kids fun run is also part of the event, with age groups for children 6 and under and ages 7–10.

Race-day details

  • 5K or 10K; the 10K is two loops of the 5K course.
  • Kids fun run with awards for participants.
  • Shower facilities are available after the race.
  • Distance changes are not available after the race starts.
