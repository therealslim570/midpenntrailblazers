Our Club

Mid Penn Trailblazers is a trail-running club based in Central Pennsylvania. We run the region’s forest paths, rocky climbs, rail trails, and state-park singletrack.

Our schedule includes established trail races and informal group runs. New runners and experienced racers are welcome; fun runs are no-drop. We’re registered and insured with the Road Runners Club of America, and giving back is part of our purpose. We support the local park community—especially R.B. Winter State Park, home to one of our races—and donate to local fire and EMS companies, Boy Scout groups, and churches.
