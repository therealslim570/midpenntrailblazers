A club for the outdoors

Since the start,
with room for everyone.

The best part of a run is who you share it with.

Mid Penn Trailblazers is a running club for people who love the adventurous outdoors. We’re primarily trail based, and welcome every experience level—from first trail miles to seasoned racers.

We organize local races and laid-back fun runs, and believe nobody gets left behind. We’re registered and insured with the Road Runners Club of America, and care about enjoying and preserving the places we run.
